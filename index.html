DrainReport is a documentation and estimating tool for licensed drain and plumbing professionals. It does not perform a NASSCO-certified pipeline condition assessment. The findings and recommendations in every report reflect the on-site judgment of the inspecting plumber, not an automated diagnosis by the app. Before authorizing major excavation or pipe replacement, homeowners are advised to obtain independent confirmation of the recommended repair.
